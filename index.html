The Failure Data Index

20 public robot failure and recovery datasets. For each one I opened the original dataset card, license file, paper and project page instead of copying a catalog.

Of the 20, 8 allow commercial use, 2 don't, 3 state no license, and 7 aren't released or aren't failure datasets. Only REBOOT is public, allows commercial use and includes executable recovery trajectories, and even it lists three different licenses. None of the 20 offers real-robot failures from deployed policies under a confirmed commercial license.

Free tools

robot-failure-schema-tools is MIT licensed and has two parts.

  • Converter. Puts failure and recovery data from CSV, Parquet or JSON into one schema, with a SHA-256 manifest. Empty fields stay empty; nothing is guessed.
  • Scorer. Compares synthetic and real failures on four axes (failure type, failure length, recovery success and recovery delay) and lists the real failure types your synthetic data never produced.
